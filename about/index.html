About KCTW

KCTW is the creative name of Melvin D. Brown III, a developer, rapper and music producer based in the Atlanta area. He is co-founder and lead developer of RARE Agency, which builds games, apps and websites for artists and businesses, and the founder of prodbyKCTW LLC.

He builds for the web: browser games, interactive 3D and apps, working mostly in React and JavaScript, Python, and Cloudflare Workers and Pages, with AI tools built on the Anthropic API.

As a musician he is a rapper and producer and a self-taught multi-instrumentalist on guitar and keys.

Divine Origins

Divine Origins is his comparative theology library. Each tradition is described in its own voice, scholarly context is kept separate, and every claim is labelled as documented, debated or the tradition's own account. The data and specification are public on GitHub.
