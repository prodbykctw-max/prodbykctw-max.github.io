Projects by KCTW

Browser games, apps and reference projects, built and maintained by KCTW, co-founder and lead developer of RARE Agency.

  • Divine Origins
    A comparative theology library: creator gods, sacred texts and calendars across the world's traditions, with photos of the original manuscripts and a 3D map of 1,742 deities.
  • CREASE
    Neon air hockey for the iPhone Duo: open it for a two-player table, fold it for a pocket table against the CPU.
  • Will Hill: Player One
    An Atlanta runner game starring rapper Will Hill, from EAV to Buckhead.
  • Jandé: Once Upon A Time
    A browser game for Jandé's R&B song: an action RPG and the Royal Runner endless run.
  • Lala Luna Land
    A free-roam 3D island adventure built for singer Lala Luna.
  • Corner Store Dash
    A browser game from EBT (Everything But Trapped): reach the corner store before the bully catches you.
  • MACRO
    A protein and meal-planning tool: targets, meal plans and supplement guides.
